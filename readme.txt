Trips, departures, destinations and trip styles for small travel companies. Works with any modern WordPress theme.

== Description ==

Journeyline gives small travel companies everything they need to show their trips:

* Trips with a story, highlights, a day-by-day itinerary, what's included and what isn't.
* Departures with dates, price per person, seats and book-by dates. Statuses update on their own: Booking open, Guaranteed, Sold out and Booking closed.
* Destinations (countries and regions) and trip styles (the kinds of journey you offer).
* Settings for your own words: Trip, Tour or Journey; your currency; your group sizes.

Journeyline is accessible, translation-ready and collects no data about your visitors.
